# 📱 Mobile Phone Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes mobile phone sales data using **Microsoft Power BI** to understand sales performance, customer preferences, pricing, discounts, ratings, and market trends.

The dashboard helps identify top-performing brands and models, explore which phone specifications are associated with higher sales, and compare brands across key business metrics.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Track total sales and sales performance by brand and model.
- Analyze the relationship between discounts and sales.
- Understand customer preferences for RAM, processor, battery capacity, and screen size.
- Compare customer ratings across brands and models.
- Explore average prices and market differences between brands.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Microsoft Power BI** — data visualization and interactive dashboard
- **DAX** — measures and calculations, where applicable
- **Data analysis** — aggregation, comparison, and interpretation of sales metrics

## 🔍 Business Questions Explored

### Sales Performance
- Which brand generated the highest total sales?
- Which model achieved the highest sales?
- What is the average sales performance by brand?

### Pricing & Discounts
- Which brand has the highest average discount?
- How does discount percentage relate to sales?
- Do higher discounts always correspond to higher sales?

### Customer Preferences
- Which RAM size has the highest sales quantity?
- How does battery capacity relate to quantity sold?
- Which processor and screen-size categories have higher sales?

### Customer Feedback
- Which brand has the highest average rating?
- Which models have the highest number of reviews?

### Market Trends
- How do brands compare in sales, average rating, and average price?

## 💡 Key Insights

The dashboard's current summary highlights the following findings. These should be interpreted in the context of the dataset and the aggregations used in the visuals:

- Realme has the highest total sales value among the brands in the current dashboard.
- Most models in the Top 10 are priced below ₹30,000, although premium models such as iPhone XR and iPhone 12 are also represented.
- 6GB RAM, Qualcomm processors, and larger screen sizes are leading configurations by sales quantity in the current dashboard.
- iPhone XR has the highest total sales value among the models in the current analysis.
- POCO has the highest average discount among the brands.
- Apple has the highest average listed price and average customer rating in the current dashboard.
- Redmi Note 4 has the highest number of customer ratings/reviews; this is review volume, not the star-rating score.
- Phones with a 4000 mAh battery have the highest quantity sold in the current dashboard.
